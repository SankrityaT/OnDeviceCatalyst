# Code of Conduct

## Commitment

OnDeviceCatalyst is committed to a respectful, welcoming, technically rigorous
community. Participation is open regardless of experience, identity,
background, nationality, appearance, disability, or personal beliefs.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss evidence plainly while treating people with respect.
- Critique ideas, code, experiments, and claims, not individuals.
- Make room for contributors who are learning.
- Give credit and respect authorship, licenses, model terms, and confidentiality.
- Report benchmarks and compatibility results honestly, including failures.
- Accept moderation decisions and help de-escalate conflict.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, stalking, or sexualized attention.
- Personal attacks, insults, trolling, or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing private information or confidential research without permission.
- Repeated unwanted contact.
- Misrepresenting results, compatibility, authorship, or security findings.

## Enforcement

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, project spaces, community
channels, and public interactions made while representing the project.

Report concerns privately through the contact method on the
[SankrityaT GitHub profile](https://github.com/SankrityaT). Maintainers may edit
or remove content, issue warnings, restrict participation, or permanently ban
participation when necessary to protect the community.

Reports will be handled as promptly and impartially as possible. Information is
shared only with people needed to understand and address the report.
